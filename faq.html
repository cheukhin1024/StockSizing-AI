What is StockSizing AI and how does it work?

+

StockSizing AI is an advanced portfolio optimization platform that uses artificial intelligence to recommend optimal asset allocation. It works by analyzing historical market data, correlations between assets, and applying modern portfolio theory to maximize returns while managing risk.

Do I need any technical skills to use StockSizing AI?

+

No technical skills required! Our platform is designed with user-friendliness in mind. Simply input your assets, select your preferred timeframe, and the system will automatically generate optimized portfolio weights for you.

Can StockSizing AI handle large-scale portfolio management?

+

Yes, our platform can handle portfolios of various sizes. Free users can optimize up to 5 assets, while premium plans allow for more complex portfolios with up to 30 assets across different asset classes including stocks, forex, cryptocurrencies, and commodities.

How does StockSizing AI improve portfolio performance?

+

StockSizing AI improves portfolio performance by finding optimal weight allocations that maximize returns relative to risk. Our AI algorithms analyze historical data and correlations between assets to create diversified portfolios that aim to deliver better risk-adjusted returns compared to manual allocation methods.

What kind of support does StockSizing AI offer if I need help?

+

We offer comprehensive support including detailed documentation, video tutorials, and direct email support via support@stocksizingai.com. Premium users also get priority support with faster response times.

What countries and stock exchanges are supported?

+

StockSizing AI supports a wide range of global markets and exchanges:

  • US - New York Stock Exchange (example: BOA)
  • UK - London Stock Exchange (example: TSCO.LON)
  • Canada - Toronto Stock Exchange (example: SHOP.TRT)
  • Canada - Toronto Venture Exchange (example: GPV.TTV)
  • Germany - XETRA (example: MRK.DEX)
  • India - BSE (example: RELIANCE.BSE)
  • China - Shanghai Stock Exchange (example: 600104.SHH)
  • China - Shenzhen Stock Exchange (example: 000002.SHZ)
  • US - NASDAQ, NYSE, and other US exchanges

Plus many more international markets across Europe, Asia, and other regions.
